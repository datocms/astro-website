# DatoCMS Brand Guidelines

How DatoCMS sounds and looks, and how other people can use our name and logo.

This is a high-level guide, not a design system. The exact values (color tokens, type sizes, spacing) are in `DESIGN.md`. The positioning and the facts we can claim are in `PRODUCT.md`.

Sections 1 to 9 are for anyone who writes or designs as DatoCMS. Section 10 is for partners, press, and plugin authors.

> Lines marked **TBC** are proposals that still need a decision.

---

## 1. In short

DatoCMS is the rare combination of deeply capable and refreshingly down-to-earth.

We know our craft, have strong opinions, and care about doing things properly. Expertise never creates distance. We share what we've learned, stay grounded, and focus on what helps people build better things.

The result is a brand that feels effortless: confident without showing off, opinionated without being preachy, and generous with what it knows.

**The litmus test: good DatoCMS work feels like advice from someone who's done this before.**

Think of the cool uncle in the Metallica shirt: deeply knowledgeable, down-to-earth, and quietly shaped by years of experience. He's seen enough to know what matters, comfortable enough not to prove it, and generous enough to share what he's learned.

## 3. The name

| Rule                                                                            | ✅ Write                              | ⛔ Not                              |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| The name is **DatoCMS**: one word, capital D, capital CMS. Product and company. | DatoCMS                               | Dato CMS · Datocms · DATOCMS · Dato |
| "Dato" alone is only for the legal entity.                                      | Dato srl                              | "At Dato we…" · "the Dato voice"    |
| Avoid the possessive. Use the name as an adjective.                             | the DatoCMS team · the DatoCMS editor | DatoCMS's features                  |
| In running text, the name is never bold, colored, or set in a different font.   | Built with Astro and DatoCMS          | Built with Astro and **DatoCMS**    |

### "We" or "DatoCMS"

- **"We" is the people.** Use it for what the team does, thinks, and promises: "We price things the way we'd want to pay for them."
- **"DatoCMS" is the software.** Use it when the system does something, in the product UI and the docs: "DatoCMS sends a POST request to this URL."
- In marketing copy, the name is a label, not the subject of the sentence.

| ⛔ Not                                              | ✅ Instead                                |
| --------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| DatoCMS provides flexible content modeling.         | Model it. Query it a second later.        |
| DatoCMS partners with leading technology providers. | We work with the tools you already use.   |
| We send a POST request to this URL. (in the UI)     | DatoCMS sends a POST request to this URL. |

## 4. Voice

The voice is constant. It is how we always sound, in every channel.

### We write with conviction

We don't hedge, soften, or hide behind corporate language.

- ⛔ "APIs, SDKs, and developer tools designed for seamless integration with any tech stack."
- ✅ "Everything the UI does, the API does too."

### We make it tangible

We turn features into outcomes people can picture.

- ⛔ "Omnichannel publishing" · "Structured content management"
- ✅ "Publish once. Every channel." · "Ask for a title. Get a title."

### We favor concrete language

- ⛔ "Transform your content operations"
- ✅ "Never chase after content ever again."

### We sound like people

- ⛔ "We are committed to delivering exceptional customer experiences."
- ✅ "We care about getting things right." · "If something breaks, you'll hear from us."

### We prefer understatement

We don't need to oversell.

- ⛔ "The ultimate content platform." · "The most complete headless CMS to work with Next.js"
- ✅ "A CMS should be boring. In a good way."

### We stay constructive

We focus on what we're for, not what we're against. Competitors are named plainly and never put down.

- ⛔ "How to Hate Your CMS Less" · "Why X falls short"
- ✅ "How to still like your CMS in two years" · "When to choose DatoCMS?" next to "When to choose X?"

### We say where we stop

Honest limits build more trust than a perfect list.

- ⛔ "Complete version history for all your content."
- ✅ "Not every version."

### Developer fluency

We speak the language because we build for the people who use it. We borrow familiar terms where they add clarity or personality, never to prove we belong in the room: technical debt, boilerplate, happy path, edge case, works on my machine, ship it. Use them sparingly.

## 5. Tone by context

The voice stays the same. The tone moves with the situation.

| Context           | Tone                                     | Example                                                                                  |
| ----------------- | ---------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Website           | Confident, clear, welcoming              | "Let's talk about your project. Tell us what you're building and we'll get back to you." |
| Product UI        | Helpful, concise                         | "Nothing here yet. Get started by adding some content."                                  |
| Error states      | Calm, collaborative                      | "Something went wrong on our side. Wait a few moments, then try again."                  |
| Documentation     | Precise, practical                       | "How DatoCMS works, in plain English."                                                   |
| Sales             | Honest, knowledgeable                    | "The more context you share, the more useful we can be."                                 |
| Social            | Human, opinionated, occasionally playful | "Yes, we're Italian. We talk with our hands."                                            |
| Product launches  | Proud, grounded                          | "That's the list. For now."                                                              |
| Customer support  | Empathetic, reassuring                   | "Thanks, we've got it. Our team will reply to your email soon."                          |
| Legal and consent | Plain, direct                            | "Can we use cookies? We use them to find the parts of the site that get in your way."    |

### In the product

The product is where people work, often under pressure. The copy is calm and complete, and it never performs.

**The rules**

- Full sentences that end with a period. Titles, buttons, and menu items have no period.
- No exclamation marks. No "Sorry", "Oops", or "Whoops". No emoticons.
- No "successfully". The past tense already says it worked.
- "Please" only when we ask for a real favor.
- No contractions in messages: "Could not", "cannot", "does not".
- An error says what happened and what to do next. It does not blame the person.

**The patterns**

| Message       | Pattern                                                             | ✅ Do                                                                                                                        | ⛔ Don't                                                                                                         |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Success       | Thing + past tense.                                                 | Asset updated.                                                                                                               | Asset updated successfully!                                                                                      |
| Error         | Could not + verb. Then the next step.                               | Could not delete the record. Try again later.                                                                                | Couldn't delete record!                                                                                          |
| Unknown error | Say it is on our side. Give a way out.                              | Something went wrong on our side. Reload the page and try again.                                                             | Whoops, this shouldn't happen… there's an error on our side :(                                                   |
| Confirmation  | Title: verb + thing + "?". Body: the consequence. Button: the verb. | **Delete record?** The record will be permanently deleted. This cannot be undone. · Yes, delete                              | **Confirm the operation?** Are you sure? This operation is not reversible!                                       |
| Empty state   | Short title. One sentence with the next step.                       | No results. Nothing matches your search. Try different keywords, or remove some filters.                                     | No results were found. Please check your query or try broadening your search.                                    |
| No permission | What the role cannot do. Who can help.                              | Your role doesn't include the permissions this area requires. Contact a project administrator.                               | Permission denied!                                                                                               |
| Plan limit    | The fact. Then the two ways forward. No sales pitch.                | You reached the record limit of your plan. The project owner can upgrade the plan, or ask our sales team for a custom quote. | …please head over to your account dashboard to upgrade the plan or contact our Sales team to get a custom quote! |

## 6. Writing mechanics

| Topic               | Rule                                                                                                          | ✅ Do                                | ⛔ Don't                           |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------- |
| Case                | Sentence case everywhere: headlines, navigation, buttons, labels. Capitals only for the first word and names. | Read the docs · Talk to sales        | Read The Docs · Core Features      |
| Headlines           | A headline that is a sentence ends with a period.                                                             | A sandbox is a branch.               | A Sandbox Is A Branch              |
| Spelling            | US English. Product names keep their own spelling.                                                            | color, organization, canceled        | colour, organisation, cancelled    |
| Oxford comma        | Always, in a list of three or more.                                                                           | images, video, audio, and documents  | images, video, audio and documents |
| Point of view       | "We" for us, "you" for the reader.                                                                            | Your code gets a typed tree.         | Users receive a typed tree.        |
| Contractions        | Yes in marketing and support. No in product messages (see section 5).                                         | We'll take it from there.            | We will be in touch soon.          |
| Exclamation marks   | None.                                                                                                         | Welcome on board.                    | Welcome on board!                  |
| Emoji               | None in the website and the product. Fine on social and in support, one at a time.                            |                                      | Welcome on board! 🎉               |
| "And" or "&"        | "And" in sentences. "&" only in a short label where space is tight.                                           | Images, video, and the media library | Fast & reliable APIs               |
| Ellipsis            | The single character "…", for loading states and actions that ask for more input.                             | Loading…                             | Loading...                         |
| Apostrophes, quotes | Curly: ’ and “ ”.                                                                                             | We’re 13 people.                     | We're 13 people.                   |
| Dashes              | No em dash in copy. Use a period or a colon.                                                                  | Off by default.                      | It's off — by default.             |
| Numbers             | Words from one to nine in prose, digits from 10 and for anything with a unit. A space before the unit.        | two hours · 13 people · 40 ms        | 2 hours · thirteen people · 40ms   |
| Currency            | Symbol first, no space, "/month".                                                                             | €149/month                           | 149 € · €199/monthly               |

### Words we avoid

Disruptive · revolutionary · game-changing · cutting-edge · next-generation · transformative · future-proof · synergy · leverage · best-in-class · ultimate · world-class · seamless · supercharge · unlock · empower · utilize · incredible · awesome.

Use with care: "powerful", "easy", "simply", "just". They usually mean the sentence has not shown anything yet.

Hedges to cut: "designed to", "aims to", "created to", "helps you to". Say what it does.

- ⛔ "Designed to help teams…" · "Aims to support…"
- ✅ "Helps teams…" · "Supports…"

## 8. Logo

The logo has two forms.

- **The wordmark**: the symbol and the name "DatoCMS", in one piece. This is the default.
- **The symbol**: the "D" alone. Use it where the wordmark does not fit (an avatar, a favicon, a badge, a narrow header) or where the name is already next to it.

The master files are `src/assets/logo/wordmark.svg` and `src/assets/logo/symbol.svg`, in the astro-website repository. Every other copy comes from these two: `npm run brand-assets` makes the files of `public/brand/`, which the page `/brand` offers. The logo files of the other repositories (`dato_symbol.svg` and `dato_wordmark.svg` in `ui-public` of web, the favicons of the cms, the dashboard, and the API) are copies: change the masters, run the script, then copy the files again.

| Rule         | Detail                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| ------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| One color    | The logo is always a single flat color.                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Which color  | On a light ground, the dark ink of the theme. On a dark ground, the accent of the theme or white.                                                                                                           |
| Fixed marks  | Where the logo stands alone with no page around it (favicon, app icon, social avatar, email), it is the dark violet symbol (Iris Deep) on the Iris purple. The bare purple symbol is for dark grounds only. |
| Clear space  | Keep a margin of the height of the symbol on every side.                                                                                                                                                    |
| Minimum size | Wordmark: 20 px high. Symbol: 16 px. Below that, don't use the logo.                                                                                                                                        |
| Ground       | A flat color with enough contrast. Never a photo, a pattern, or a busy picture.                                                                                                                             |
| In a circle  | Never the symbol alone. Use the avatar file: it has a margin, and the D is moved to where it looks centered.                                                                                                |

### Don't

- ⛔ Don't draw it in two tones, such as a faded "CMS".
- ⛔ Don't add a gradient, a shadow, an outline, or any effect.
- ⛔ Don't use the old orange logo. It is retired.
- ⛔ Don't stretch, rotate, crop, or redraw it. Don't retype the name in a font.
- ⛔ Don't change the space between the symbol and the name, or stack them.
- ⛔ Don't put it in a sentence in place of the name.
- ⛔ Don't make the symbol part of another logo or icon.
- ⛔ Don't crop the symbol in a circle yourself. The D has two corners on the left and a round side on the right: with its box in the center of a circle it looks pushed to the left, and the corners touch the edge. The avatar file corrects it.

The one exception is the pixel version of the symbol, which we draw ourselves on share images.

## 9. Look

The principles only. `DESIGN.md` has the values.

### Color

- **A brand of several themes, not of one color.** Six pairs, each with a bright accent, a deep ground, and a pale paper: Pollen on Espresso, Ember, Fern, Iris, Glacier, Bubblegum.
- **One theme for each page.** The header, the hero, and the footer share it.
- **Purple (Iris) when there is room for one color only.** See the fixed marks in section 8. The product also defaults to purple.
- **No gray.** A neutral is the deep color of the theme, made lighter.
- **Dark text on a bright accent**, never white text on it. Text meets WCAG AA contrast.
- ⛔ Don't use the old orange as a brand color. ⛔ Don't mix accents of two themes in one section.

### Typography

- **ABC Oracle** for everything people read. **Geist Mono** for code and small technical labels.
- **Two weights**, a regular and a stronger one. No bold, no italic for emphasis.
- **Big headlines are tight and in two tones**: the claim in the full color, the aside in a muted one.
- **Capitals only for small mono labels.**
- ⛔ Don't add a third typeface. ⛔ Don't set a headline in capitals or in Title Case.

### Imagery

- **We draw the product, we don't screenshot it.** The interface in a picture is real product code, with invented content.
- **Invented content is believable**: a fake brand with real-looking records, never "Lorem ipsum" or "Acme", never a real customer's data.
- **Text faces** such as `[••]` are our character. One face for a moment (a hero, the end of a page), never a row of them, never typed in a sentence.
- **Pixel art** for hands, figures, and the pixel version of a picture.
- **Real people in real photos**: the team and the customers. No stock photos of people, no AI pictures.
- **Customer logos** are in one color, the color of the text. **Partner logos** keep their colors on a white plate.

### Shape and surface

- **Flat.** Solid fields of color with hard edges. A thin line or a change of ground separates two areas.
- ⛔ No gradients, no soft shadows, no glows, no blur as decoration.
- **Icons are thin line icons** from one set (Lucide). Brand marks of other companies come from Simple Icons.

## 10. Using our brand

For partners, customers, press, and plugin authors. We are happy when you show that you work with DatoCMS. These rules keep it clear who made what.

### The name

- Write **DatoCMS**, as in section 3.
- Describe your relation to it: "built with DatoCMS", "a plugin for DatoCMS", "works with DatoCMS".
- ⛔ Don't start the name of your product, company, or domain with "DatoCMS" or "Dato". ✅ "Acme Translator for DatoCMS". ⛔ "DatoCMS Translator".
- ⛔ Don't say or suggest that we made, sponsor, or endorse your work, unless we agreed to it in writing.
- "Official" is ours. Only plugins and starters that we publish carry it.

### The logo

- Use the files of [datocms.com/brand](https://www.datocms.com/brand), in the dark or the white version, and follow section 8.
- For a profile picture or any place that a circle cuts, use the avatar file.
- You may use the logo to say that you use, support, or integrate with DatoCMS: a "built with" section, an integrations page, a slide, an article about us.
- ⛔ Don't use the logo, or the symbol, as the icon of your plugin, app, or account.
- ⛔ Don't make our logo larger or more visible than your own.

### Side by side with another logo

- Put the two logos on one line, with the same visual weight, and a thin vertical line or a "×" between them.
- Keep the clear space of section 8 around each logo.
- Each logo keeps its own rules. Ours is in one color. Yours keeps your colors.
- On our website, your logo is on a white plate so that its colors stay correct on every theme.
- ⛔ Don't merge the two into one mark.

### Badges **TBC**

| Badge              | Who can use it                                         |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| Official plugin    | Us only. It marks the plugins that we maintain.        |
| DatoCMS partner    | Agencies in the partner program, while they are in it. |
| Technology partner | Companies with a listed integration.                   |
| Built with DatoCMS | Anyone with a project that runs on DatoCMS.            |

The badge files need to be drawn. Until then, use the wordmark with the text.

### Buttons

"Deploy with DatoCMS" and "Clone with DatoCMS" are buttons that we host, for a README or a docs page. Embed them from our URL and don't redraw them:

- `https://dashboard.datocms.com/deploy/button.svg`
- `https://dashboard.datocms.com/clone/button.svg`

Each button is one file: the dark symbol and the label on the Iris purple, the pair of the fixed marks. It reads on a light and on a dark ground, thus a README needs no second file for dark mode. The forms of [Project templates](https://www.datocms.com/docs/general-concepts/project-starters-and-templates) in the docs make the link and the code for your project.

### Press

Use "DatoCMS" for the product and the company. The legal entity is Dato srl, based in Italy. For anything else, write to us.
