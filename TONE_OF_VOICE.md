# Dato Tone of Voice Guide

> **In short**
>
> Dato is the rare combination of deeply capable and refreshingly down-to-earth.
>
> We know our craft, have strong opinions and care deeply about doing things properly. Expertise never creates distance. We share what we've learned, stay grounded and focus on what genuinely helps people build better things.
>
> 👉 The result is a voice that feels effortless: confident without showing off, opinionated without being preachy and generous with what it knows.

---

## Voice attributes

### ✨ Effortless

We make complex things feel simple. Confidence comes from experience, not performance. We don't show off what we know, we put it to work.

| ✅ We are                           | ⛔ We are not                       |
| ----------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| Comfortable, capable, clear-headed. | Complicated, showy, self-important. |

### 🧠 Independent

We have opinions and we're comfortable expressing them. Not because we're contrarian, but because we've earned them.

| ✅ We are                              | ⛔ We are not                     |
| -------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- |
| Self-assured, principled, opinionated. | Defensive, dogmatic, provocative. |

### 🤲 Generous

We share what we've learned. We explain things clearly, help people succeed and never use expertise to create distance.

| ✅ We are                           | ⛔ We are not                             |
| ----------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| Helpful, approachable, open-handed. | Gatekeeping, patronising, self-important. |

### 🌱 Thoughtful

We care about the people using the product as much as the product itself. Every decision is designed to make complex work feel simpler, clearer and more approachable.

| ✅ We are                   | ⛔ We are not                             |
| --------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| Warm, honest, approachable. | Corporate, performative, overly familiar. |

## How we write

### ✍️ We speak as "we"

- ⛔ **Not:** "DatoCMS provides..."
- ✅ **Instead:** "We built..." · "We believe..." · "We think..."

### ✍️ We write with conviction

We don't hedge, soften or hide behind corporate language.

- ⛔ **Not:** "Designed to help teams..." · "Aims to support..." · "Created to enable..."
- ✅ **Instead:** "Helps teams..." · "Supports..." · "Works with..."

### ✍️ We make it tangible

We translate features into outcomes people can picture.

- ⛔ **Not:** "Omnichannel publishing" · "Structured content management"
- ✅ **Instead:** "Publish once. Every channel." · "Stop starting over."

### ✍️ We favor concrete language

- ⛔ **Not:** "Transform your content operations"
- ✅ **Instead:** "Never chase after content ever again."

### ✍️ We sound like people

- ⛔ **Not:** "We are committed to delivering exceptional customer experiences."
- ✅ **Instead:** "We care about getting things right." · "If something breaks, you'll hear from us."

### ✍️ We prefer understatement

We don't need to oversell.

- ⛔ **Not:** "The ultimate content platform."
- ✅ **Instead:** "The boring stuff matters."

### ✍️ We stay constructive

We can be opinionated without being negative. We focus on what we're for, not what we're against.

- ⛔ **Not:** "How to Hate Your CMS Less" · "The Problem With Modern Software"
- ✅ **Instead:** "How to Still Like Your CMS in Two Years" · "The Joy of Boring Technology"

## Boring × Brilliant

Dato lives at the intersection of both. We build solid infrastructure, then give it a point of view.

| Boring          | Brilliant        |
| --------------- | ---------------- |
| Stable          | Flexible         |
| Reliable        | Thoughtful       |
| Consistent      | Adaptable        |
| Deliberate      | Inventive        |
| Predictable     | Surprising       |
| Infrastructural | Human            |
| Expert          | Generous         |
| Confident       | Humble           |
| Long-term       | Forward-thinking |
| Powerful        | Effortless       |

### What this looks like

Each line lands because it holds both truths at once:

> **Infrastructure with people skills.**
>
> Boring: infrastructure you can trust.
> Brilliant: it still feels human.

> **We've been running this show for over a decade.**
>
> Boring: over a decade.
> Brilliant: it's a show that we keep improving.

> **The boring stuff matters.**
>
> Boring: reliability and consistency.
> Brilliant: the confidence to say it plainly.

> **Hello and welcome to your last CMS migration.**
>
> Boring: migrations and maintenance.
> Brilliant: a long-term promise you can believe.

## Language

### ⛔ Language we avoid

- Disruptive
- Revolutionary
- Game-changing
- Cutting-edge
- Next-generation
- Transformative
- Future-proof
- Synergy
- Leverage
- Best-in-class

### Developer fluency

We speak the language because we build for the people who use it. We occasionally borrow familiar terminology and cultural references where they add clarity, credibility or personality, but never to prove we belong in the room.

#### 🗣️ Language from our customers

Phrases and ideas that show up in Casual Chats.

- mental model
- bare bones but structured
- mimic the design in data
- heavy lifting
- work for us
- static content and dynamic flexibility
- typesafe environment
- source of truth

#### 🧠 Cultural references we can borrow

Developer shorthand that technical audiences use.

- technical debt
- spaghetti code
- code smell
- refactoring
- boilerplate
- happy path
- edge case
- works on my machine
- ship it

> 💡 Use sparingly. The goal is familiarity, not performance.

## Tone in action

The examples below show how the Dato voice transforms existing website copy, demonstrating how the same product story can feel more human, confident and distinctive through language alone.

### Example 1: contact page

**⛔ Current website**

> GET IN TOUCH WITH US
>
> **Contact our Sales team**
>
> Whether you're a potential user with questions about our product, or are already using DatoCMS and have questions about expanding your current package, we're ready to help.
>
> Fill out the form below and one of our sales expert we'll be in touch as soon as possible.
>
> **Filling all the fields helps us giving you a more informed answer with less back and forth.** Also helps you thinking about your project in terms that are useful for us to understand what you are up to.

**✅ Reimagined copy**

> GET IN TOUCH
>
> **You've got questions. We have answers.**
>
> We've spent more than a decade helping teams manage content without making a mess of it.
>
> Tell us what you're working on and we'll take it from there.
>
> **The more context you share, the more useful we can be.** It usually saves everyone a few emails.

### Example 2: GraphQL

**⛔ Current website**

> THE HEADLESS CMS GRAPHQL REVOLUTION
>
> **Ask for what you need, get exactly that**
>
> Apps using GraphQL combined with a headless CMS are **fast and stable** because it is not the server which controls the data they get, _they_ are.
>
> Contrary to the REST API dogma, a **GraphQL query to your API gets exactly what you ask for**, nothing more and nothing less, that is why **GraphQL queries always return predictable results**. Read more about the advantages of the GraphQL API on their official website

**✅ Reimagined copy**

> GRAPHQL
>
> **Predictable. In a great way.**
>
> Ask for a **title**. Get a title.
> Ask for an **image**. Get an image.
> Ask for exactly what you need and nothing you don't.
>
> That's what makes GraphQL **efficient**, **predictable** and surprisingly **easy to work with**.
>
> [See for yourself]
